### Users, Wallets, and Apps

Use [Flashbots Protect](https://protect.flashbots.net/) to transact securely through BuilderNet and receive refunds for your transactions. See the [Protect documentation](https://docs.flashbots.net/flashbots-protect/overview) for more details.

For advanced users: send orderflow directly to a BuilderNet node. See the advanced [documentation](send-orderflow) for more information.

### Searchers and Solvers

Submit bundles to the Flashbots Bundle Relay or [directly to BuilderNet](send-orderflow). Learn more about how to optimize your bundles for refunds [here](https://collective.flashbots.net/t/refund-rule-wat-dis-how-to-and-faq/4049) and find historical data for your signer [here](https://app.hex.tech/9eb1e790-53f7-4c16-be76-4a22c1aa7d17/app/0c2d34ef-1304-481a-b3d6-b773ce9e0e19/latest).

### Market makers (propAMMs)

If you operate a proprietary AMM (propAMM), stream your quote updates to BuilderNet as [priority updates](api#priority-updates). Depending on the mode set for your API key, the builder places an update at the top of the block or includes it only when a transaction in the block emits a consumption event registered for your key. Updates are sent over WebSocket and go through the [Priority Update Registry](https://github.com/flashbots/priority-update-registry). API keys are issued on request; message the team on Telegram (see below).

Aggregators and solvers that route against propAMM liquidity can read the pending quote state from the [quote stream](api#state-stream-). No key is needed.

### Developers

Contribute to [rbuilder](https://github.com/flashbots/rbuilder), the open source block builder BuilderNet is based on. Join the conversation in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/flashbots_rbuilder).

---

### Researchers

Contribute to the security and design of decentralized block building through initiatives like [zero trust execution environments](https://writings.flashbots.net/ZTEE), applications of software cryptography in MEV (eg. [MPC](https://writings.flashbots.net/backrunning-private-txs-MPC), [FHE](https://writings.flashbots.net/blind-arbitrage-fhe)), and more on our [research forum](https://collective.flashbots.net/c/research/20). Apply for grants from teams like Flashbots and the PBS Foundation to research new proposals like [TEE-Boost](https://collective.flashbots.net/t/tee-boost/3741). We are interested in establishing robust benchmarks for decentralization that can be used to measure BuilderNet’s progress and bring transparency to the community, and welcome input on the [forum](https://collective.flashbots.net/).
